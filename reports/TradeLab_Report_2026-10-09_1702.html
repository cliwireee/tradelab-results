Methodology & data sources

How strategies are tested

Walk-forward testing: settings are chosen on an earlier stretch of data, then the strategy is scored on the following stretch it has never seen, repeated across 4 windows (3 where history is short) and stitched together. Settings are chosen by average result per trade, never by win rate. Every trade enters at the next candle's open; if a stop and a target are hit in the same candle, the stop is assumed.

Costs

  • Crypto: 0.10% fee per side plus 2–5 basis points slippage per side (by timeframe).
  • Gold: 0.05% per side (spread + commission) plus 1–1.5 bp slippage per side and 0.005% per day financing; position size may reach 5× the account (risk still capped at 1% per trade).

Position sizing & risk rules

Quarter-Kelly sizing from past closed trades, capped at 1% of the account at risk per trade; new trades pause after a 3% daily loss or a 20% drop from the peak.

Data

  • Crypto: Binance public market data (no account), 7 coins, 5-minute to daily candles.
  • Gold: COMEX gold futures (Yahoo symbol GC=F, unadjusted continuous contract, so contract rolls/glitches can cause jumps). 5- and 15-minute history is only ~60 days. Cross-checked against PAXG (a gold-backed token on Binance).
  • Portfolio: daily BTC-USD, QQQ (Nasdaq-100) and GLD (gold) via Yahoo Finance.

Range of outcomes

2,000 bootstrap resamples of each setup's tested trades; it shows luck, not regime changes, outages or liquidity.

Things to keep in mind

  • Headline setups are the best of many tested combinations, so their results are optimistic.
  • Gold breakout's gains came mostly from long trades during a strong gold bull market.
  • Paper accounts started on 9 Oct 2026 and have too few trades to judge.
  • Simulated results only. Not financial advice. Past and backtested performance does not predict future results.
