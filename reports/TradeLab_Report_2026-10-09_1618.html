Caveats

  • PAPER TRADING / RESEARCH ONLY: simulated fills, public market data, no exchange keys, no real orders.
  • All backtest figures are walk-forward OUT-OF-SAMPLE after fees, slippage (and carry for gold). Setups marked † were chosen as the best of many cells, so they are selection-biased — expect worse live results.
  • Crypto costs: 0.10% fee/side + 2–5 bps slippage/side. Gold: 0.5 bp fee/side + 1–1.5 bps slippage/side + 0.5 bp/day carry; gold notional may reach 5x equity (risk still capped at 1%/trade).
  • Gold data = Yahoo GC=F COMEX futures (unadjusted continuous contract: roll/glitch jumps exist, e.g. −7.7% bar on 2026-02-02); 5m/15m history only ~60 days, so short-TF gold results are anecdotal. PAXG used as cross-check. Intraday Yahoo data may lag ~10 min.
  • Gold breakout's edge came mostly from long trades during a strong gold bull market; it roughly matched or trailed buy & hold on return, with much smaller drawdowns.
  • Monte Carlo resamples past OOS trades; it does not model regime change, liquidity or execution outages.
  • Win rate alone is misleading: several high-win-rate setups (e.g. mean reversion) lose money after costs.
  • Paper accounts started on 2026-10-09 and have too few trades to judge yet.
